Shared check pool

This MacBook shares the same planned check pool as the iMac fleet. There is no separate public-download quota just for this device: at most one check is delegated to the MacBook at a time, and the fleet as a whole runs at most three checks concurrently. Raw daily check totals stay configured on the extension monitor page and are unchanged by this page.
